What is a QR Code?

QR (Quick Response) codes are two-dimensional barcodes that can store various types of data including text, URLs, contact information, and file links. They can be scanned using smartphone cameras or dedicated QR code readers, making them perfect for bridging physical and digital experiences.

Best Practices

  • Test Before Printing: Always test QR codes before mass printing
  • Adequate Size: Ensure QR codes are at least 2x2 cm when printed
  • High Contrast: Use dark QR codes on light backgrounds
  • Error Correction: Use higher error correction for printed materials
  • Clear Space: Leave white space around QR codes for better scanning
  • Test Distance: Test scanning from expected user distances

Troubleshooting

QR Code Won't Scan

  • Ensure adequate lighting when scanning
  • Check if QR code is damaged or obscured
  • Try different angles and distances
  • Clean camera lens
  • Use higher error correction when generating

File Upload Issues

  • Check file size is under 10MB
  • Verify file format is supported
  • Ensure stable internet connection
  • Try uploading a different file
  • Clear browser cache and try again

Camera Not Working

  • Grant camera permissions in browser settings
  • Check if camera is being used by another app
  • Try using a different browser
  • Restart your device
  • Use the image upload option instead

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are QR codes free to create?
A: Yes! Our QR code generator is 100% free with unlimited usage.

Q: Do QR codes expire?
A: Static QR codes (text/URL) never expire. File-based QR codes depend on Cloudinary's storage retention.

Q: Can I edit a QR code after creating it?
A: No, QR codes are static. You need to generate a new QR code for different content.

Q: What's the maximum data capacity?
A: QR codes can store up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters, though smaller amounts scan more reliably.

Q: Can I use QR codes commercially?
A: Yes! QR codes are not proprietary and can be used for any purpose including commercial use.

Q: Do I need an app to scan QR codes?
A: Most modern smartphones can scan QR codes directly with their native camera app, or you can use our web-based scanner.
